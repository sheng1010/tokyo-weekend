About Tokyo Weekend

Tokyo Weekend is a small guide to films, exhibitions, nightlife, and city activities. Listings are collected from public museum, cinema, and event pages, then shortened for the site.

An event with an end date before today is hidden. A film that only says “now showing”, with no end date, is not labeled as currently showing. “This weekend” means Friday through Sunday of the current week, and only events whose dates overlap that window.

Dates, venues, and ticket links come from the source pages. The short summaries are editorial text. This site does not invent visitor scores or reviews.
